# Analytics Design

## 1. Dashboard

### Total Sales Value
- **Business_question**: What is the total sales amount?
- **Metric**: Total Sales Value
- **Source_table**: `order_items`, `orders`
- **Required_Columns**:
    - `orders.order_status`
    - `order_items.price`
- **Grain**: 1 row = entire dataset
- **Output**: `Total sales value` = total
- **Filters**: Exclude cancelled orders

### Order Quantity
- **Business_question**: How many orders are there in total?
- **Metric**: Total Orders Number
- **Source_table**: `orders`
- **Required_Columns**: `orders.order_id`
- **Grain**: 1 row = entire dataset
- **Output**: `Total orders` = total
- **Filters**: None

### Total Customers
- **Business_question**: How many customers are there in total?
- **Metric**: Total Customers Number
- **Source_table**: `customers`
- **Required_Columns**: `customers.customer_id`
- **Grain**: 1 row = entire dataset
- **Output**: `Total customers` = total
- **Filters**: None

### Best 5 Selling Product Categories
- **Business_question**: What 5 product categories have the most units sold?
- **Metric**: Best 5 Selling Product Categories
- **Source_table**: `order_items`, `products`
- **Required_Columns**:
    - `order_items.product_id`
    - `products.product_id`
    - `products.product_category_name`
- **Grain**: 1 row = one product_category
- **Output**:
    - `Product category name` = name
    - `Total unit sold` = total
- **Filters**: None
- **Ranking**: Top 5 categories by total unit sold
